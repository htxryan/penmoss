---
title: Quick capture
description: Jot something down from anywhere on the Mac or the phone, into today's note or a new note in the Inbox.
---

This page shows you how to capture a thought without opening a note: from any app on the Mac with a hotkey, and from the share sheet, a tile or a widget on Android.

A capture goes to one of two places:

- **Today's Note:** added under a `## Captures` heading in [today's note](/docs/journal/), with the time in bold, such as `**14:32** Call the plumber`.
- **New Note in Inbox:** a new note in the Inbox folder, titled from its first line.

The default is set in **Settings → Daily Notes → Quick Capture** (on Android, **Settings → Quick Capture**). The capture form lets you pick the other one for a single capture. Captures go into your [Primary Notebook](/docs/organise/#the-primary-notebook); with several notebooks, **Into** in the form chooses another.

Captures are saved on the device at once, offline too, and synced later.

## On the Mac

Press **⌃⌥⌘N** in any app. A small capture window opens over it, even over a full-screen app.

- Type, paste or drop a picture, then **Save** (⌘Enter).
- Escape cancels. Clicking elsewhere hides the window and keeps what you typed for next time.

Inside Penmoss, **Quick Capture…** (⌘⇧K) opens the same form. The [menu bar icon](/docs/mac/#the-menu-bar-icon) has **Quick Capture** too.

### Change the hotkey

Set it in **Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts → Quick capture from any app**, then **Save**. It needs two modifiers or more, one of them ⌘ or ⌃, and not a combination that copies, pastes, undoes or edits text. macOS cannot tell Penmoss whether another app takes the keys first, so if the hotkey does nothing, choose another.

## On Android

- **Share sheet:** share text, a link or pictures from any app to **Penmoss**. A shared web page becomes its title and address.
- **Quick Settings tile:** add the **Capture** tile, and tap it to open the capture sheet with the keyboard up.
- **Home-screen widget:** add the **Capture** widget.
- **Launcher shortcut:** long-press the Penmoss icon and choose **Quick Capture**.

The sheet, **Capture to Penmoss**, has the same two places. With no notebook yet, it says to add one first.
