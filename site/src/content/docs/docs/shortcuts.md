This page shows you how to run any command from the keyboard, the default shortcuts on the Mac, and how to change them.

## The command palette

**⌘K** opens the command palette. Type part of a command or a note's title, then Enter. With nothing typed it shows your five most recent notes, then every command by group. Every action in Penmoss is a command, so everything in the menus is here too, with its shortcut.

Two more ways to get around:

- **Quick Open Note…** (⌘P): open a note by its title.
- **Switch to a Recent Note** (⌘E): hold ⌘ and press E to step through recent notes, add Shift to step back, and let go to open one.

**Keyboard Shortcuts** (⌘/) shows every shortcut on one sheet.

## Change a shortcut

Open **Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts** (or **Customise** on the shortcut sheet):

1. Click a command's shortcut.
2. Press the keys you want. Escape cancels.
3. If another command already has them, Penmoss says which, and **Use It Here Instead** moves them.

**Remove** takes a shortcut away, and **Reset to the Default** brings back the original. A shortcut needs ⌘, Ctrl or ⌥ (on a Mac, not ⌥ alone with a letter, which types a character). A few keys are kept for the system and text editing, such as ⌘C, ⌘V, ⌘Z, ⌘F, ⌘W and ⌘Q. Your shortcuts sync to your other devices.

The [quick capture hotkey](/docs/quick-capture/#change-the-hotkey) is set on the same page.

## Default shortcuts on the Mac

On Android with a hardware keyboard, Ctrl takes the place of ⌘; **Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts** there lists the keys.

### In the sidebar

With a folder focused: F2 renames it, Delete deletes it, and ⌥↑ and ⌥↓ move it.

Many commands have no shortcut until you give them one, such as **Keep on Top**, **Move to Folder…**, **Send Feedback** and every **Insert: …** block.
