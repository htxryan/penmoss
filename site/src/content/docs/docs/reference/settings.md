This page lists every setting, page by page, so you can find one and see what it does.

Open Settings with ⌘, on the Mac, or from the **☰** menu on Android. Most settings are kept in your [Primary Notebook](/docs/organise/#the-primary-notebook) and so reach your other devices; the ones marked with a laptop stay on the device where you set them (hover over the laptop, or focus it, to read why). Settings that belong to a notebook itself (its time zone and templates) are the same on every device and agent that opens it.

### Kept on each device

The look and layout stay on the device where you set them, and never reach a notebook, so a theme or light/dark mode picked on one Mac never changes another:

- **Appearance:** the mode, the theme, the fonts and the note text size, Show Tooltips After and Dad Jokes.
- **Sidebar** (**Home** on Android): its shortcuts, which sections show and in what order, Notes in Recent Notes, and Quick Access's Show Notebook Names and Notes in the Sidebar.
- **Note:** Note Width, the title bar's buttons, and what shows under the title and in the status bar.

They are marked with a laptop. **Copy Settings…** and **Keep Settings in Step** leave them as they are. Until you change one on a device, it shows what that device showed before. Your custom themes (to pick on any device), Quick Access pins, symbols, Daily Notes and Quick Capture stay the same everywhere.

### Kept apart for your Macs and your phones

The keyboard shortcuts are kept once for your Macs and once for your Android phones. Every Mac shares the Macs' and every phone the phones'. On the Mac they are marked with a monitor; on Android, with a phone.

## On the Mac

Settings lists its pages in five groups: **Look and Feel**, **Writing**, **Notebooks**, **Agents** and **This Mac**, with **Search Settings** at the top of the list. Nothing under This Mac is synced; the heading says so once, so its pages don't repeat it.

Each page opens with a small **Settings ⓘ** over its title and a line about it. Hover over the ⓘ, or focus it, to see which notebook your settings are synced through, with a link to its page. As you scroll down, the title moves into a bar pinned at the top: **Settings ⓘ / Appearance**.

### Search Settings

The field over the list finds a setting by its name, its help, or another word for it ("typeface" or "text size" for the fonts). While it has words in it, the matches take the page's place, each with the page and group it's on. Click one, or press Return for the first, to open its page with the setting marked; press Esc to clear the field. ↓ and ↑ move between the field and the matches.

Old links still work: Settings → Display opens Sidebar, and Settings → Status opens GitHub.

## Look and Feel

Appearance, Sidebar and Note each have two tabs: **Settings**, which the page opens on, holds the settings below, and **Preview** shows what they change, as large as the page allows. Searching for a setting opens its page on the Settings tab. On Android, Appearance, Home and Note have the same two tabs.

### Appearance

| Setting                   | What it does                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mode                      | Light, Dark, or System (the default), which follows macOS                                                                                                         |
| Theme                     | The theme; **Customize…** copies one to make your own ([Themes and fonts](/docs/themes/))                                                                         |
| Interface Font, Note Font | The fonts over the theme's own                                                                                                                                    |
| Note Font Size            | 12 to 28 px                                                                                                                                                       |
| Show Tooltips After       | How long the pointer rests on a control before its tooltip shows: 0.5, 1 (the default), 1.5 or 2 seconds, or Never. Keyboard focus always shows a tooltip at once |
| Dad Jokes                 | A dad joke as the heading when no note is open; on by default. Off, the heading reads Welcome to Penmoss                                                          |
| Compact Title Bar         | The window's buttons in Penmoss's own top row; on by default (this Mac)                                                                                           |

### Sidebar

| Setting                             | What it does                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shortcuts                           | Which of Tags, Today, This Week, Graph and Commands the sidebar's top row shows (Search always shows); all are on by default |
| Sections                            | The order of Quick Access, Recent Notes, Notebooks, Calendar and Tags                                                        |
| Notes in Recent Notes               | 1 to 50, 10 by default                                                                                                       |
| Show Notebook Names in Quick Access | Labels each pinned note with its notebook                                                                                    |

### Note

| Setting           | What it does                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Note Width        | The theme's width, or Full Window                                                                                                           |
| Note Panel        | Whether the note panel starts Closed or Open (this Mac)                                                                                     |
| Title Bar Buttons | Which actions have a button at the end of the tab strip (beside the title in a note window): Full Width and Copy Agent Reference by default |
| Under the Title   | Which of notebook, folder, dates, tags, words, characters, reading time and token count show there                                          |
| In the Status Bar | The same for the status bar; words, characters and reading time by default                                                                  |

### Symbols

The [line symbols](/docs/editor/#line-symbols): each one's key, glyph (from the symbol picker: symbols and emoji, searched by name) and colour (an emoji keeps its own colours, so its colour can't be changed), their order (drag a row, or Alt+↑ and Alt+↓, which changes its key), **Add** (a symbol, the Checkbox, a Bulleted List Item or a Numbered List Item; up to nine in all) and **Restore Defaults**.

### AI Descriptions

| Setting           | What it does                                                                       |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Describe Notes    | On or Off for every note whose note, folder and notebook say Inherit; Off at first |

## Notebooks

**All Notebooks** lists every notebook with **Add Notebook…** (the same steps as [your first notebook](/docs/first-notebook/#more-notebooks-on-this-mac)), then each notebook has an entry of its own in the list, with its [badge](/docs/organise/#notebook-badges) and a dot for how its sync is going. A notebook's page, in its **Overview**, **Sync** and **Git** tabs:

| Setting                              | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| AI Descriptions                      | On **Overview**: Inherit, On or Off for this notebook's notes, and **Describe Notes Now…** ([AI Descriptions](/docs/ai-descriptions/))                                                                             |
| Sync Now                             | Syncs at once                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Notebook Name, Rename                | Renames it on every device that syncs it ([Rename a notebook](/docs/organise/#rename-a-notebook))                                                                                                                  |
| Badge                                | On **Overview**, after its name: its colour (ten presets or **Custom…**), what it shows (a letter, a number from 0 to 99 or an icon) and **Reset to Default** ([Notebook badges](/docs/organise/#notebook-badges)) |
| Repository                           | Where it syncs, as its remote names it. **Open on GitHub** (or **Open in Browser**) opens it, an SSH host alias from `~/.ssh/config` included                                                                      |
| Push after (seconds)                 | How long after your edits settle it pushes: 12 by default                                                                                                                                                          |
| Pull every (minutes)                 | How often it pulls while Penmoss is open: 1 by default                                                                                                                                                             |
| Sign commits as git is configured to | Untick to never sign this notebook's commits                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Commit name, Commit email            | This notebook's own commit identity, over git's (a GitHub no-reply address keeps yours private)                                                                                                                    |
| Access                               | **Save Token** and **Forget Token**, for an HTTPS host that needs a token                                                                                                                                          |
| Remove from This Mac                 | Removes this Mac's copy, after Touch ID; the repository keeps everything pushed                                                                                                                                    |

On All Notebooks, under **Between Notebooks**, with **Primary Notebook** first:

| Setting                                          | What it does                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary Notebook                                 | The notebook that holds your journal, Inbox and these settings (this Mac)                                     |
| Keep Settings in Step, Copy Settings…            | Copies your app settings to your other notebooks, once or every time they change                              |
| Copy Tags When Copying Notes to Another Notebook | Whether **Copy Tags** starts ticked in [Copy to Notebook…](/docs/organise/#notebooks)'s picker; on by default |

## Agents

### Agents

**The MCP Server**: the one command for every notebook, for Claude Code (with **Receive Comments Sent to Claude Code Sessions**) and Codex, with **Copy** ([Connect an agent](/docs/agents/connect/)). For each notebook, its agent copies: the one Penmoss made, which the server uses, and older copies connected by name, with **Remove**. **The penmoss Command** installs the CLI ([Install](/docs/reference/cli/#install)).

### Agent Activity

What agents changed, in every notebook, with Undo ([Agent activity](/docs/agents/activity/)).

## This Mac

### Semantic Search

**Semantic Search**, on by default; then what it's doing, with a progress bar while the model downloads or your notes are indexed, and the model with its size and licence. Turning it off stops it and keeps the model. **Delete Model…** asks first, then deletes the model and its index from this Mac and turns it off; turning it on again downloads the model again. See [Search](/docs/search/#semantic-search).

### GitHub

The GitHub accounts Penmoss uses, kept in this Mac's Keychain, each with **Sign Out**, and **Use for Send Feedback** on all but the first. **Sign In with GitHub** (**Add Account** once there is one) or **Use a Token Instead…** adds another, such as a work account. Under **Used For**: adding notebooks from your repositories, and **Send Feedback**, with how many reports are waiting and **Open**. See [More than one GitHub account](/docs/first-notebook/#more-than-one-github-account) and [Send feedback](/docs/help/feedback/).

### Updates

A card with the version you're running, whether it's up to date, the channel and when Penmoss last checked, and **Check Now** (or **Install and Restart**). Under it, **Open Source Notices** with **Show Notices**, which shows the licences of the open source software Penmoss includes, such as [Lucide](https://lucide.dev)'s icons. Then **Update Automatically** (off by default) and **Update Channel** (Stable or Alpha), and **What's New**, folded away until you open it (open by itself for an offered update or after one): what an offered update brings, what this version brought, and earlier versions. See [Updates](/docs/help/updates/#whats-new).

### Privacy

What Penmoss records about how you use it, on this Mac, and whether it is shared. See [Usage data](/docs/help/usage-data/).

- **Share Usage Data**: sends the record to Penmoss's developer from now on. Off, it stays on this Mac.
- **View Analytics Data**, with the counts by state: opens **Analytics Data** under Privacy, every event recorded by the app, the CLI and the MCP server, newest first, 100 at a time, filtered by App, CLI or MCP and by state, with **Copy as JSON**.
- **Analytics ID**: the random ID your events carry, with **Reset My Analytics ID** and **Delete My Shared Data** (which asks first).
- **Logs**: **Show Log File** shows the app's log in Finder.
- **Advanced**, folded away: how long events are kept (**Keep Events For**, **Keep at Most**), the logs' size and age (**Log File Size**, **Log Files Kept**, **Keep Log Lines For**), and what a bug report attaches (**Attach Logs from the Last**, **At Most** lines and KB), each with its range, and **Reset to Defaults**. Lowering one that would delete anything asks first.

## On Android

Open Settings from the **☰** menu. Each screen's title is large at the top and shrinks into the bar as you scroll; the ⓘ in the bar says which notebook your settings are synced through. The same five groups as the Mac, with **This Phone** in place of This Mac, and a **Search Settings** field at the top that opens the screen a setting is on, scrolled to the setting and marked for a moment. On **All Notebooks**, **Between Notebooks** starts with **Primary Notebook**, then has **Keep Settings in Step** and **Copy Settings…** as on the Mac (with two notebooks or more), and Keep Settings in Step is this phone's own choice.

| Writing       | Keyboard Shortcuts | Shortcuts for a hardware keyboard                                                                                                                          |

## For a notebook, from the CLI or an agent

`penmoss settings` and the `get_settings` and `set_settings` tools read and change a notebook's own settings: `timeZone`, `template.daily` and `template.weekly`. They cannot change the apps' settings.
