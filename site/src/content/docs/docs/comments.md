This page shows you how to leave comments on your notes, reply to them and resolve them.

Comments are kept with the note in your notebook, not in its markdown, so they sync to your other devices but do not change the `.md` file.

## Comment on a passage

Select some text and choose **Comment** in the formatting toolbar, or press ⌘⌥⇧M. Type your comment and send it with ⌘Enter. The passage is highlighted; click it to open its thread.

To comment on the note as a whole, choose **Add Note Comment** in the Comments tab.

## The Comments tab

**Show or Hide Comments** (⌘⌥⇧A) opens the note panel's **Comments** tab. It lists the note's threads in the order of the note, with **Open** and **Resolved** filters. In each thread you can:

- **Reply**.
- **Resolve** it when it is done, or **Reopen** it.
- **Delete Thread**.

A thread whose passage has been deleted is listed last.

## Send a comment to an agent

You can send a thread to an AI agent's session, such as Claude Code, which reads it, edits the note and replies in the thread. Choose the session in the thread's **Send to** menu; the thread then says whether it was sent, seen and answered. See [Comments to agents](/docs/agents/comments/) to set it up.

## On Android

The note's ⋮ menu has **Comments**, and the selection menu has **Comment**. Tap a highlighted passage to open its thread.
