Dark mode was the page's `prefers-color-scheme` (`emulateMedia`), because the app's own mode is synced. - **Console and network.** Errors, warnings, `pageerror`, failed requests and 4xx/5xx responses were collected in every run: **none**, on the web in Chromium, WebKit and the phone layout, or on the Mac.

## Bugs found and fixed

Each was confirmed by hand before and after its fix.

1. **Tooltips near the right edge wrapped onto two lines** (`Comments (⌘⌥⇧C)`, `Note Actions`). The tip was measured where its control is, so a fixed box near the edge shrank to fit before it was placed. It is now measured at the window's corner. `apps/web/src/shell/Tooltip.tsx`. With jsdom there is no layout, so no unit test; checked in Chromium and WebKit (one line, 132 × 25 px). `web-05-comments-tooltip-light.png`.
2. **The Rounded font drew every word in bold in Chrome and the Mac app.** Chromium has no `ui-rounded`, and the next face that exists on every Mac is `'Arial Rounded MT Bold'`, which has only a bold weight. Bold text could not be told apart from the rest. CDP's platform-fonts probe showed `Arial Rounded MT Bold` in Chromium and in Electron. With the face dropped from the stack it shows `.SF NS`. `packages/core/src/fonts.ts`; `packages/core/test/fonts.test.ts` ("names no face that has only a bold weight") failed before the fix. `web-16-bug-rounded-bold-before.png`.
3. **Title Case misses (A938).** The not-found pages' link said "Back to your notes"; Settings already said "Back to Your Notes". The symbols' Spaces hint named the section "Keyboard shortcuts". `apps/web/src/App.tsx`, `apps/web/src/notes/NoteRoute.tsx`, `apps/web/src/notes/symbols.ts`, and `apps/web/test/e2e/smoke.spec.ts` for the link's new name.
4. **The lightbox's Full Screen did nothing in the Mac app.** The page's permission handler refused `fullscreen`, and Electron never settles a refused `requestFullscreen()`, so the button hung silently. Native full screen worked. `fullscreen` is now allowed. `apps/desktop/src/security.ts`, with `apps/desktop/test/unit/shell.test.ts` updated. After the fix, both `document.fullscreenElement` and `isFullScreen()` are true, the first Escape leaves full screen, and the second closes the lightbox. `mac-20-lightbox-fullscreen-dark.png`.

Tests: web 669, core 453, desktop 186, all passing; Prettier is clean on the changed files. The web and Electron Playwright suites were not run: they need the ports `task dev` holds.
